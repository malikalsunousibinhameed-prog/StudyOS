About StudyOS

A small tool for keeping schoolwork organized.

Why it exists

StudyOS is designed to make it easier to remember deadlines, decide what to work on next, and keep a basic record of focused study time. It keeps the workflow intentionally simple: add assignments, review what is due, and mark work complete.

What it includes

  • Assignment creation, editing, completion, and deletion.
  • Search, status filters, and deadline sorting.
  • A short Today's Mission list based on urgency and priority.
  • A 25-minute study timer and recorded study sessions.
  • Assignment totals and a seven-day study activity chart.

Built with

Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Jinja templates.

StudyOS is a local, single-user learning project. It does not include user accounts or cloud synchronization.
